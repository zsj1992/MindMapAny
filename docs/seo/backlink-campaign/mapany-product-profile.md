## 长简介（约 600–900 字符，目录表单用）

> MindMapAny is an AI-powered mind map generator that turns long content into structured, editable, source-traceable mind maps. Paste text or upload a PDF, DOCX, EPUB, PPTX or web article, and get a real hierarchy — not a shorter summary, but a map of the content you can edit and verify against the original. Every node traces back to the source: page numbers for PDFs, slide numbers for slide decks, chapter titles for ebooks. Long documents are summarized section by section and merged into a stable hierarchy of up to 5 levels (110 nodes max), with duplicate topics collapsed and orphan nodes dropped. The map is fully editable (rename, add child/sibling, collapse), exports to PNG, SVG or Markdown, and can be shared via a public link without sign-up. Output language is independent of source language (30+ languages), and a deep-research mode builds multi-source reports with citations. Free plan with 30 credits; paid plans via Creem. Made for people who need to read, verify and organize long content carefully.

## 功能清单（用于表单字段，按需截取）

1. 7 种输入：长文本、PDF、DOCX、EPUB、PPTX、网页链接、深度研究问题
2. 节点级溯源：PDF 页码 / PPTX 页码 / EPUB 章节 / 网页段落锚点
3. 稳定层级：按章节切块→归纳→合并，去重折叠，最多 5 层、110 节点
4. 生成后可编辑：双击改名、Tab 加子节点、Enter 加同级、Space 折叠
5. 导出：PNG / SVG / Markdown；公开分享链接（免登录查看）
6. 30+ 输出语言，源语言与输出语言相互独立
7. 深度研究：DeepSeek 研究计划 + 网络检索 + 带引用多来源报告
8. 免费额度：新账号 30 credits；套餐按积分计费
