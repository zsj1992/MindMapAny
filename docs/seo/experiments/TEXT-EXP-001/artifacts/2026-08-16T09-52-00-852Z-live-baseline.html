Frequently asked questions

How much text can I paste at once?＋

The limit depends on your plan and the generation depth you choose — see the pricing page for the exact character allowance. Longer content costs more credits.

Does it handle text that mixes languages?＋

Yes. You can specify the output language separately and the node language will be made consistent.

Can I add child nodes afterwards?＋

Yes. Select a node and press Tab for a child node, or Enter for a sibling.
